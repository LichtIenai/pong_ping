POLYGONPONG

One arena. Everyone owns a side. Lose your paddle, lose a life — last side standing wins.

Camera
How to play
  • The arena is a polygon — every player defends one side with a paddle that slides along it.
  • Ball past your paddle: you lose a life. No lives left: you are eliminated.
  • Eliminated? You get 2 obstacle charges: a Vortex that whips the ball away at random speed, or a Barrier that rebounds it.
  • Hosts set lives and how fast the ball speeds up the longer nobody concedes.
  • Move with the mouse/finger, or ← counter-clockwise and → clockwise.
